# Approval Policy

## Review Order

Review the oldest submissions first. When items share a submission date, review safety-critical content (safety documents and equipment or sensor specifications) before operational content (onboarding material, zone layouts) and then training media such as demo videos. An item that blocks site operations moves to the front of the queue regardless of its date.

## Rejection Criteria

Reject an item when the file is corrupt or cannot be opened, when required checklists or metadata are missing, when the content is an outdated version, or when it exposes sensitive data such as personnel details or credentials. Reject anything that conflicts with current safety standards. Every rejection must include a written reason so the submitter can correct and resubmit.

## Escalation

Escalate to the Safety Lead when an item affects safety procedures or equipment specifications. Escalate to the Site Manager when it changes zone layouts or patrol routes. If you are unsure after one full pass, escalate instead of approving. Never approve an item you could not fully review.

## File-Type Rules

Folder: open every file inside; approval applies to the whole folder, and an empty folder is rejected. Video: watch it end to end and confirm the audio and the on-screen steps match the written procedure. PDF: confirm the page count, the version number and that no pages are missing or redacted. Image: confirm labels and legends are legible and that no faces or private information are visible.

## SLA

Standard items are reviewed within two business days of submission. Safety-critical items are reviewed within one business day. Escalated items receive a response within four hours. Anything past its deadline is flagged as overdue and goes to the top of the queue.
